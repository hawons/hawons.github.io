Email / CV / Google Scholar / LinkedIn / GitHub

I am an AI Research Engineer at Makinarocks, working on large language models and vision-language models.

My research centers on how reliably we can evaluate language models. I have studied how pairwise LLM judges amplify biased preferences rather than reveal them, and how multi-reference evaluation makes automatic assessment more robust. Broadly, I am interested in evaluation that still holds up outside the benchmark it was designed for.

I received my M.S. at KAIST AI, advised by Jaegul Choo at DAVIAN Lab, and my B.S. in Statistics at Ewha Womans University. Before joining Makinarocks, I interned at NAVER Cloud as a Research Scientist.

Feel free to reach out if you would like to discuss research :)
